One performer, one session, one machine. Protocol and metric definitions:
[MEASUREMENT.md](MEASUREMENT.md). The take rules and the scripted sequence
were committed before the takes they apply to (`ff2ce20`, `46dad68`).

## Clips

| Condition | Take | Length | Intended gestures | Recorded live with |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| normal | `s1-normal-scripted` | 171.2 s | 20 | pre-gap (`main.py` sha256 `3c17c266…`) |
| fast | `s1-fast-scripted-2` | 142.6 s | 30 | current (`4bed89aa…`) |
| occlusion | `s1-occlusion-scripted` | 166.1 s | 34 | current |
| lowlight | `s1-lowlight-scripted` | 158.4 s | 28 | current |

Total 10.6 minutes, 112 intended gestures. All four are scripted takes of
the pre-registered set `R1 L1 R2 R3 L2 R4 L3 R5 R1 L5 R2 L2 L4 L1`.

- **normal**: the performer restarted after six gestures (`R1 L1 R2 R3 L2
  L3`), then played the set once. Both passes are scored.
- **fast**: the set twice as quick flicks. In pass 1 the performer added
  `L3 R3` after `R3` (performer's account).
- **occlusion**: the set twice with a drumstick held between thumb and index
  finger, which covers the thumb. Pass 1 has `R5` where `R3` was meant (the
  performer's own pose), then `R3` and `L5` to correct it; pass 2 has extra
  `L3 R3` and `R1 L1` (performer's account).
- **lowlight**: the set twice with room lights off and the laptop screen as
  the only light. No lux reading was taken. Median frame brightness was
  43/255, against 109/255 in the three normal-light takes. The camera
  delivered about 17 frames/s here (median frame interval 60 ms), against
  about 30 in normal light.

The first `fast` take (99.9 s, 20:42) is not scored. When quick flicks did
not register, the performer redid them slowly, so the take does not follow
the sequence. It was used to develop the tracking-gap change. The `idle`
clip was optional and was not recorded.

Time before the first and after the last gesture is excluded from tracking
loss (1 s margin), and in the normal take also the pause between its two
passes.

| fast | 9.84% (297 of 3019 frames) | 8.4 |

The normal take was recorded before the rule that the hand stays in view,
and the performer lowered the hand while waiting for loop boundaries
(checked on sampled frames). Its 61.8% of frames without a hand is mostly
that, not tracking loss.

**Live, current version** (from the takes' own frame logs, same scoring):
every intended gesture fired live in all four takes (the normal take with
the pre-gap version, the others with the current one). Replay of the same lowlight clip gives 25/28. Replay reads the
compressed recording, and in low light that changes some detections. For
the fast pass 1 and the occlusion take the intended sequence is the
performer's account given after seeing the app's responses, so the live
figure for those is not independent.

**Software latency** (live, separate run with `--log` only, current
version): 14 loop starts from silence, frame read → `play()` call, median
8.2 ms, 90th percentile 9.0 ms, range 7.8–9.0 ms. Median frame interval
in that run 33.3 ms. This leaves out camera capture, audio output and the
time a swipe takes to cross the speed threshold. End-to-end
gesture-to-sound latency was not measured.

**Live frame interval during the takes** (two encoders running): median
33–35 ms in normal light; 90th percentile 66–67 ms in the normal and fast
takes, 36 ms in the occlusion take; median 60 ms in low light.

## What these numbers do not support

- **One performer, one session, one machine and camera.** The protocol asks
  for at least three sessions; there is one.
- **Not independent of the performer's practice.** The performer practised
  with the pre-gap and current versions and recorded three of the four takes
  with the current version's live feedback. Gestures were made the way the
  current version accepts them. The baseline was never practised and is
  likely disadvantaged.
- **The tracking-gap change was developed on this session's material**: the
  unscored first fast take, an older practice session and the R1 idle clip.
  Its 4 widths/s limit was chosen from those. It was not tested on clips
  recorded independently of its development.
- **No false-trigger rate.** No idle clip was recorded. Extras in scripted
  takes rose from 1.41 to 2.35 per minute with the tracking-gap change.
- **Low light is still the weakest condition** (89.3% in replay, 4.17
  extras per minute). Nothing in the code is specific to low light; the gain
  comes from the general swipe changes.
- **Occlusion was tested with one object and one grip.** The other hand
  crossing in front and the hand at the frame edge were not tested.
- **Not measured**: end-to-end latency, the volume pinch, the head nod,
  other sound sets, other cameras, resolutions or lighting setups.
